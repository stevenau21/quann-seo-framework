## Query Template Patterns (from SEO RAG methodology)

### a. Entity + Attribute + Value
`{Entity} with {Attribute} {Value}`
→ "homes in Katy with pool", "condos in Houston with 3 bedrooms"

### b. Attribute + Entity
`{Attribute} {Entity}`
→ "affordable homes Houston", "luxury homes Katy", "new construction Austin"

### c. Value as Modifier + Entity
`{Value} {Entity}`
→ "$300k homes Katy", "4-bedroom houses Houston", "investment properties Texas"

### d. Location + Entity + Attribute
`{Entity} in {Location} with {Attribute}`
→ "homes in Katy under 400k with pool", "condos in Houston with garage"

### e. Question-Based
`{Question word} {Entity} {Attribute}?`
→ "how much down payment first home Texas?", "what are best neighborhoods Katy families?"

### f. Comparison
`{Entity} vs {Entity}` or `{Entity} with {Attribute} vs {Attribute}`
→ "Katy vs Houston suburbs", "FHA vs conventional loan Texas"

### g. Intent-Driven
`{Intent verb} {Entity} {Attribute}`
→ "buy first home Texas", "sell home Katy", "invest rental property Houston"

### h. Long-Tail Compound
`{Entity} that {Attribute} {Value} and {Attribute} {Value}`
→ "homes in Katy that allow pets and have fenced yard"

## Instantiated Templates from Quan's EAV

### From Entity: "First-Time Home Buyer"
- `{buy first home in} {Houston}` → "buy first home in Houston"
- `{first time home buyer} {programs} {Texas}` → "first time home buyer programs Texas"
- `{FHA loan} {requirements}` → "FHA loan requirements Texas"
- `{how much} {down payment} {first home}` → "how much down payment for first home"
- `{first time buyer} {mistakes}` → "first time home buyer mistakes to avoid"
- `{closing costs} {Texas} {first time buyer}` → "closing costs Texas first time buyer"

### From Entity: "Out-of-State Relocation"
- `{moving to} {Houston} {from} {California}` → "moving to Houston from California"
- `{relocating to} {Texas} {guide}` → "relocating to Texas guide"
- `{Texas} {property tax} {vs} {California}` → "Texas property tax vs California"
- `{buying} {home} {sight unseen} {Texas}` → "buying home sight unseen Texas"

### From Entity: "Katy, TX"
- `{homes for sale} {Katy} {under} {400k}` → "homes for sale Katy under 400k"
- `{best neighborhoods} {Katy} {families}` → "best neighborhoods in Katy for families"
- `{Katy} {school district}` → "Katy ISD schools"
- `{new construction} {Katy}` → "new construction homes Katy TX"
- `{Katy} {vs} {Houston}` → "living in Katy vs Houston"
