## Credentials

Quan holds five professional designations that define his approach to buyer representation.

**ABR — Accredited Buyer's Representative.** The ABR designation requires advanced training in buyer advocacy, negotiation strategy, and fiduciary duty. Less than 15% of REALTORS® earn this certification. It is the core credential for an agent whose practice is 85% buyer-focused.

**GRI — Graduate, REALTOR® Institute.** The GRI designation demands 90+ hours of education across contracts, finance, marketing, and professional ethics. It signals comprehensive mastery of real estate practice beyond basic licensing requirements.

**C2EX — Commitment to Excellence.** Endorsed by the National Association of REALTORS®, C2EX verifies performance across ten competency areas: client service, market knowledge, negotiation, professionalism, and ethical conduct. It is earned, not purchased.

**MRP — Military Relocation Professional.** The MRP certification equips Quan to serve relocating military families and veterans — a skill set that applies directly to his out-of-state buyer specialization. VA loan timelines, PCS orders, and military relocation benefits are integrated into his relocation process.

**PSA — Pricing Strategy Advisor.** The PSA designation provides advanced training in comparative market analysis, property valuation, and offer pricing strategy. Quan applies this training to every offer his clients submit — not just to listings.

## The Quantum Team System

Every buyer Quan represents moves through the same four-stage process.

**Stage 1: Pre-Approval.** Quan connects buyers to a network of five Texas lenders. Each lender provides a Loan Estimate within 48 hours. The buyer selects one and locks their rate. Quan does not recommend lenders — he presents the data and lets the buyer choose.

**Stage 2: Neighborhood Matching.** For Katy buyers, Quan's neighborhood matching process scores 12+ communities against the buyer's criteria: school zone, commute time, MUD tax rate, HOA fee structure, flood zone classification, price band, and new construction availability. Out-of-state buyers receive the same analysis before they board a plane.

**Stage 3: Offer Strategy.** Quan applies his PSA training to every offer. The strategy accounts for list-to-sale ratio in the target ZIP, days on market, competing offers, appraisal gap probability, and seller motivation signals. The offer is data-backed, not emotional.

**Stage 4: Close.** From the option period through the final walkthrough, Quan manages the transaction timeline. Inspections are scheduled within 72 hours of contract execution. TREC forms are completed same-day. The closing disclosure is reviewed line-by-line with the buyer before signing.

## Get Paid to Buy

Quan's signature differentiator. The average Quan buyer receives $8,500 in builder incentives and closing cost credits.

The strategy stacks three negotiation levers:

1. **Builder Incentives.** Quan tracks builder incentive calendars across Katy, Fulshear, and Richmond. Quarter-end, month-end, and model closeout periods produce the largest concessions. Incentives include closing cost credits, rate buy-downs, upgrade allowances, and structural option credits.
2. **Lender Credits.** Quan's lender network competes on rate and closing cost structure. When a builder offers a preferred lender incentive, Quan compares it against independent lender quotes to determine whether the "preferred" deal actually benefits the buyer.
3. **Seller Concessions.** On resale transactions, Quan negotiates seller-paid closing costs, home warranty coverage, and repair credits during the option period.

The three levers are non-exclusive. Quan has closed transactions where all three fired simultaneously — producing $15,000+ in captured value for a single buyer.
