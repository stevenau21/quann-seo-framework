## What Is the Information Gap?

Per the methodology: if you only write what people are already searching for, you are an **imitator**. If you cover the entire information graph — including concepts nobody knows to search for — you are an **authority**.

These are "zero search volume nodes" — concepts that tools show as 0 volume but are **logically necessary** to explain the topic completely.
