## Bridge Template


## Principles

1. Only build bridges for pages that actually exist or are committed
2. Bridge comes at the END of the page, after value delivered
3. No pressure — acknowledges their journey, offers Quan as natural next step
4. One bridge per page. Not scattered throughout.
