## What Is the Consensus Baseline?

Per the methodology, search engines don't know "the truth" — they know **consensus**. The **consensus baseline** is what every credible source agrees on. The **truth range** is the legitimate variation among authoritative voices. Content that only repeats the baseline is thin. Content that explores the truth range demonstrates deep expertise.

| Consensus Baseline | Truth Range |
|---|---|
| FHA: 3.5% minimum; Conventional: 3-20%; VA: 0%; USDA: 0% | TDHCA programs can cover down payment → effective 0%. Some lenders offer 1% down conventional. Builder incentives. Credit score affects minimum. |

### "What are closing costs in Texas?"

| Consensus Baseline | Truth Range |
|---|---|
| Typically 2-5% of purchase price. Includes lender fees, title insurance, escrow, prepaids. | Varies by county (Harris vs Fort Bend). New construction has different cost structure. Seller concessions can reduce buyer burden. MUD taxes increase prepaids. |

### "How long does it take to buy a home?"

| Consensus Baseline | Truth Range |
|---|---|
| 30-45 days typical closing | New construction: 6-12 months. Cash: 7-14 days. VA loans: often 45+. Competitive market with appraisal gaps: unpredictable. |
