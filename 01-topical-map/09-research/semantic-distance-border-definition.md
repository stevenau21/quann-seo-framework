## Danger Zone: What Happens If You Cross Borders Without Bridges

Per methodology:

1. **Ranking Signal Dilution:** If the search engine sees your site covering seller content, investment content, AND buyer content equally, it cannot identify your primary focus. The centerpiece annotation — "this is a buyer's agent site" — weakens.

2. **Competitive Disadvantage:** A site that focuses ONLY on Texas luxury buying will outrank Quan on a luxury buying query because it has 100% focus on that topic, while Quan's site is split 3 ways.

3. **E-E-A-T Confusion:** If Quan writes about investing but isn't certified as a financial advisor, the content may create an E-E-A-T conflict. Agent license ≠ investment advisor credential.

**Rule:** Build Distance 0-3 content first. Only expand to Distance 4-5 after Quan confirms, and only with explicit contextual bridges. Do not publish gray zone content without a bridge paragraph that explicitly connects it back to buyer representation.
