## What This Means for the SEO Strategy

### Advantage
- **Clean slate** — no inherited toxicity, no negative ranking state
- **No redirect complexity** — nothing to resurrect, nothing to clean up
- **Full topical control** — search engine associates the domain ONLY with what we build

### Disadvantage
- **No aged domain authority** — a 10-year-old real estate domain with 50 backlinks starts with more trust
- **No legacy anchor text** — cannot leverage existing "Quan Nguyen realtor" or "Katy TX agent" links
- **Sandbox period possible** — newer domains may take longer to establish trust signals

### Mitigation Strategy
1. **Build entity signals aggressively** — GBP, Zillow, HAR.com profiles must link to quann.homes immediately
2. **Backlink acquisition starts at launch** — Chamber of Commerce, HAR member directory, REAL BROKERAGE directory
3. **Structured data from day one** — JSON-LD helps Google recognize the entity without needing age signals
4. **Content velocity matters more for new domains** — publish 6-8 pages in first month to establish topical footprint quickly
