# Phase 2 Reference: Pre-Execution Blueprint Methodology

**Source:** Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR — Holistic SEO & Digital
**Date Saved:** 2026-05-04

## Summary

This phase covers six architectural prerequisites that must be completed before writing content:

1. **Topical Borders & Distances** — Define where authority starts and ends. Adjacent topics must be evaluated for semantic closeness vs dilution risk.

2. **Information Gap** — Identify zero search volume nodes that are logically necessary to explain the topic fully. If you only write what people search for, you're an imitator. Cover the entire information graph = authority.

3. **Consensus Baseline & Truth Range** — Search engines know consensus, not truth. Establish ground truth from .gov, .edu, and industry associations. Content must align with consensus baseline while exploring the truth range.

4. **Contextual Bridges** — Every informational page (Outer Section) must have a logical pathway to the monetization page (Core Section). The bridge is the sentence that answers "now what?"

5. **Lexical Richness** — Document vocabulary must exceed query vocabulary. Use Knowledge Domain Terms that prove expert authorship — language a novice wouldn't know to search.

6. **Cost of Retrieval Architecture** — Segment information into tables, lists, paragraphs strategically. Use semantic HTML to lower Google's computational cost of understanding your site. A "cheap" site to process outranks a "noisy" one.

## Key Quotes

> "Before the first word is written or the first digital asset is deployed, you must transition from a keyword-seeking mindset to one of Conceptual Universe Mapping."

> "If you only write what people are already searching for, you are an imitator; if you cover the entire information graph, you are an authority."

> "A site that is cheap for Google to understand will always outrank a noisy and expensive competitor."
