## What Is Lexical Richness?

Per the methodology: your **Document Vocabulary** must be richer than your **Query Vocabulary**. A first-time buyer types "help buying house" — your page must contain the language of an expert agent, not just "we help you buy a house." Using specialized Knowledge Domain Terms proves the content was created with human effort and deep expertise, not AI-generated summary of common knowledge.

## Quan's Knowledge Domain Terms

### Texas-Specific Terms (buyers from other states don't know)

| Term | Plain English | Why It Proves Expertise |
|---|---|---|
| Option Period | Due diligence window — buyer pays small fee for right to terminate | Unique to Texas contracts |
| MUD Tax | Municipal Utility District tax — pays for neighborhood infrastructure | Katy-specific, surprises out-of-state buyers |
| TREC Forms | Standardized contracts from Texas Real Estate Commission | Shows knowledge of regulatory framework |
| Homestead Exemption | Property tax break for primary residence in Texas | Every Texas homeowner should know this |
| PID | Public Improvement District — another special tax district | Common in new construction communities |
| Survey (T-47) | Texas-specific survey affidavit for title | Out-of-state buyers never expect this cost |

### Contract & Transaction Terms

| Term | Plain English | Why It Proves Expertise |
|---|---|---|
| Earnest Money | Good faith deposit held in escrow | Basic but buyers misuse the term |
| Escalation Clause | Automatic bid increase if another offer comes in | Shows competitive market strategy knowledge |
| Contingency | Condition that must be met for contract to proceed | Inspection contingency, financing contingency, sale contingency |
| Due Diligence | Investigation period for buyer | Option period is Texas's version of this |
| Seller Concession | Seller paying some of buyer's closing costs | Negotiation tool buyers don't know they can ask for |
| Appraisal Gap | Difference between offer price and appraised value | Increasingly critical in Texas markets |
| Specific Performance | Legal remedy if seller backs out | Shows deep contract knowledge |
| Estoppel Letter | HOA document confirming no unpaid fees | Hidden closing cost nobody warns about |
| Proration | Splitting costs (taxes, HOA) at closing between buyer/seller | Detail that separates agent from order-taker |

### Mortgage & Financing Terms

| Term | Plain English | Why It Proves Expertise |
|---|---|---|
| DTI (Debt-to-Income) | Percentage of income going to debt | Critical qualification metric |
| PITI | Principal + Interest + Taxes + Insurance = real monthly payment | Buyers think mortgage = P&I only |
| Rate Lock | Freezing an interest rate for a period | Float vs lock strategy |
| Origination Fee | Lender's upfront charge | Comparison shopping term |
| Discount Points | Pay upfront to lower rate | Math that buyers need explained |
| PMI / MIP | Mortgage insurance — PMI (conventional), MIP (FHA) | FHA MIP now permanent — devastating detail |
| Underwriting | Lender verifying borrower qualification | The mysterious "black box" of mortgage approval |
| Loan Estimate | Standardized 3-page disclosure of loan terms | Required by law, most buyers can't read it |
| Closing Disclosure | Final loan terms, must be accurate within tolerance | 3-day rule before closing |

### Investment Terms

| Term | Plain English | Why It Proves Expertise |
|---|---|---|
| Cap Rate | Net Operating Income / Property Value | Core investment metric |
| Cash-on-Cash Return | Annual cash flow / cash invested | Better metric than cap rate for leveraged properties |
| NOI | Net Operating Income — revenue minus operating expenses | Foundation of all investment analysis |
| Cash Flow | What's left after ALL expenses including mortgage | Most important number for investors |
| BRRRR | Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat | Investment strategy framework |
| 1031 Exchange | Defer capital gains tax by rolling into new investment | Sophisticated investor tool |
| Depreciation | Tax deduction for property wear over time | Huge tax advantage only investors get |
| LTV | Loan-to-Value ratio | Bank's risk metric |

### Market Analysis Terms

| Term | Plain English | Why It Proves Expertise |
|---|---|---|
| CMA | Comparable Market Analysis — pricing method | Not just "look at Zillow" |
| DOM | Days on Market | But also days on market manipulation |
| Absorption Rate | How fast inventory sells | Real measure of buyer/seller market |
| Months of Inventory | Homes for sale / monthly sales rate | 6 months = balanced. Under 3 = seller's market |
| List-to-Sale Ratio | Final price as % of list price | Measures negotiation leverage |
| Price Per Square Foot | Value / sqft | Misleading without adjustment for lot size, upgrades |
| Seasonality | Market activity by month | Texas real estate has distinct seasons |
| Walk Score | Walkability metric | Increasingly matters to young buyers |
