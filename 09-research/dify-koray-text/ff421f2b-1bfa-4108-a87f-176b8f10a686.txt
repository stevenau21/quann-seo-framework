The terms “me,” “myself,” and “I” are English words that are used interchangeably in a sentence. These words are often misused because they all refer to the same thing. The words “me,” “myself,” and “i” refer to “oneself.”  These terms are all considered pronouns. Pronouns are words substituted for other nouns in cases where the nouns are already mentioned or described in the previous sentences. The differences between the words “me,” “myself,” and “i” come in spelling, pronunciation, and roles in the sentence. The term “me” is spelled out with the letters “m” and “e.” The word “myself” is spelled out by combining the two words “my” and “self.” “I,” on the other hand, consists of only one letter. The words “me,”  “myself,”  and “i” perform particular functions in the sentences. The term “I” acts as a subject pronoun, “me” behaves as an object pronoun, and “myself” functions as a reflexive or intensive pronoun.
The differences and comparison of the words “me”, “myself”, and “i” are shown below.
“Me” is used to refer to himself or herself as the object of a verb or preposition.
“Me” is used in exclamations.
“Me” is used as an objective pronoun.
“Myself” is used to refer to himself or herself as the object of a verb or preposition when the word “I” is the subject of the clause.
“Myself” is used reflexively for emphasis or in absolute constructions.
“Myself” is used as a reflective pronoun.
“I” is used to indicate the ninth letter of the English alphabet.
“I” is used to refer to the one who is speaking or writing in a first person, singular form.
“I” is used as a subject pronoun.
Writers and speakers must recognize the similarity and distinction of misused terms to know how to decide to use the words “me”, “myself” or “i”. These words are distinct in terms of  spelling and pronunciation but  have the same purpose. However, the terms “”me”, ”myself” or ”i “ have different roles to perform in a sentence, in spite of their similarities as a pronoun.  “Me” is a pronoun applied as an object or receiver of the action. The word “myself” on the other hand, is enforced in sentences to reflect the noun. Furthermore, The term “i” is utilized as the subject of the pronoun.

What does “Me” Mean?
“Me” is a word used to refer to himself or herself as the object of a verb or preposition. It is sometimes used to indicate an exclamative expression.  The word “me” is a pronoun which replaces the name of a person or thing in a sentence. It is the objective form of the word “I”. “Me” originated from the word mē during the Old English era. Its first known use is recorded during the 15 century where an erroneous and vulgar use of nominative (such as “it is me”) was proven. However, the word “me” began to replace “I” sometime around the 16th century largely because of the pressure of word order. The term “me” is defined as  the form of I that is used when the speaker or writer is the object of a verb or preposition, or after the verb be, according to the Oxford Learner’s Dictionaries. It is a very common word in English because it is enforced in normal conversations and sentence construction. A lot of people use the word “me” as a replacement for the noun in an objective format  to create a  variation in the sentence or the speech. The term “Me” is an important word for daily life because it helps not just writers and authors but ordinary individuals as well to express their thoughts in an easier way.
