Jeffrey Ding is the author of the ChinAI Newsletter. Jeffrey Ding is an Assistant Professor of Political Science at George Washington University, specializing in the intersection of technological change and international politics. Jeffrey Ding previously served as a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford’s Center for International Security and Cooperation, sponsored by Stanford’s Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence.
Jeffrey Ding’s research focuses on the impact of previous technological revolutions on the undulation of great powers, with a specific emphasis on US-China competition in emerging technologies such as AI. Jeffrey Ding has undertaken substantial studies on the identification of strategic technologies, national scientific and technological capabilities, and interstate cooperation on nuclear safety and security technologies. Jeffrey Ding’s research has been or appeared in prestigious academic journals such as the European Journal of International Security, Foreign Affairs, Review of International Political Economy, and Security Studies. Jeffrey Ding’s research has been cited in notable news outlets such as The Washington Post and The Financial Times.
