Google Search Central - SEO Starter Guide

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
Type: official_documentation
Confidence: confirmed

SEO is about helping search engines understand your content, and helping users find your site. Google is a fully automated search engine using crawlers to explore the web. Google primarily finds pages through links from other pages it already crawled.

Key guidelines from Google:

1. Help Google find your content: submit a sitemap, ensure pages aren't blocked. Check if Google can see your page the same way a user does. Don't hide CSS and JavaScript.

2. Organize your site: use descriptive URLs with words useful for users. Group topically similar pages in directories. Reduce duplicate content - search engines choose a single canonical URL per piece of content.

3. Make your site interesting and useful: create compelling, useful content. Text should be easy-to-read and well organized. Content should be unique - don't copy others, create original content. Content should be up-to-date - check and update previously published content. Content should be helpful, reliable, and people-first. Expect your readers' search terms - anticipate what users search for.

4. Avoid distracting advertisements that prevent users from reading content.

5. Link to relevant resources. Write good link text (anchor text) that tells users and Google what linked pages contain.

6. Optimize images: use alt text, descriptive filenames, proper formats.

7. Make your site mobile-friendly. Use responsive design.

8. Promote your website. Effective promotion of new content leads to faster discovery.

9. Analyze search performance and user behavior. Use Search Console and Analytics.

Important: Every change takes time to reflect - some hours, some months. Not all changes result in noticeable impact. Iterate if unsatisfied.

Key principle: Content created with users in mind, not search engines. People-first content.
